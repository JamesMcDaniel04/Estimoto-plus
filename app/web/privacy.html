Privacy policy

Updated September 19, 2026

Estimoto Plus is a private garage for your vehicles, estimates, repairs and service history. This policy explains what the app stores, who can see it and how you stay in control. It applies to the Estimoto Plus mobile and web apps and the customer service that supports them.

What you give us

Sign-in. Your email address is your identity. Sign-in codes and links are handled by our authentication provider, Supabase; we never see or store a password.

Profile. Your name, phone number, ZIP code and preferred contact method, which you can edit or clear in Settings.

Garage. Vehicle year, make, model, nickname, mileage, VIN, insurer and policy number when you choose to save them, plus an optional private vehicle photo.

Estimates and requests. Damage descriptions, claim details, guided-capture photos, VIN label photos, service requests and the shop you selected.

Service history. Repair, maintenance and modification records, costs, and the receipt photos or PDFs you attach. Receipt totals and line items are read on our own servers to fill in costs; that text is not sent to any outside service.

Shops, reminders and lookups. Your private shop contacts, scheduling requests, reminders, dedicated-shop choices and vehicle value lookups.

Activity notices. When a shop replies, an estimate is ready, a time is confirmed or a reminder comes due, we record a notice you can read in the app. If you keep Email me about updates on in Settings, the same notice is emailed to your sign-in address through the Resend email service. You can turn those emails off at any time.

Diagnostics. If the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, it sends an anonymous report: the error type, a short message, the first lines of the technical trace, the app version and platform. Email addresses, phone numbers and sign-in tokens are removed before storage, the report is not linked to your account, and reports are deleted after 30 days.

What we share, and only when you ask

Nothing in your garage is visible to a shop, technician or other customer until you take an explicit action. Each action shows a review screen first.

  • Submitting an estimate or service request sends your name, contact details, vehicle summary, description and the photos you attached to the participating Estimoto shop you selected, through the Estimoto shop service.
  • Scheduling with one of your own shops emails that shop the request you approved. Delivery uses the Resend email service.
  • Google Calendar, when the connection is offered and you turn it on, is linked through Nango. We store calendar identifiers and the time slots you chose, never your Google password.

Services that help the app work

  • Shop search sends the search ZIP, vehicle make, selected service and search text to OpenStreetMap, Zippopotam.us and, where enabled, Google Maps. Your name, email, VIN, photos and receipts are never included. See the shop search privacy notice.
  • Representative vehicle images and value estimates come from CarsXE using your vehicle's year, make, model and, for values, mileage, condition and state. Your VIN and identity are not sent.
  • Estibot answers may be improved by an AI model. Only your question and the vehicle's year, make, model and mileage are sent, and the model is instructed not to store them. Contact details, VIN, insurance, account and shop data never enter the model.

We use no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, we do not sell your information, and we do not use your private records to train models. On your phone, the app keeps a private copy of your last loaded garage so it can open offline; that copy is removed when you sign out or delete your account. If you opt in to aggregated insights in Service history, only coarse, de-identified maintenance patterns are combined with other opted-in customers; you can opt out at any time and the setting is off by default.

Where your data lives

Account identity is stored by Supabase. Your garage, records and private files are stored on Estimoto servers hosted in the United States, encrypted in transit and reachable only with your signed-in session. Photos, receipts and other files are kept on a private volume that is not publicly addressable.

Your controls

  • Edit or remove anything from the app: vehicles, photos, estimates, requests, records, receipts, shops, reminders and lookups.
  • Download my data in Settings gives you a complete JSON copy of your account.
  • Delete my account in Settings permanently erases your profile, every record and every private file, cancels open shop requests and removes your sign-in. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. A shop that already received a request keeps the copy you chose to send it.

Data is kept until you delete it or delete your account. Bounded technical counters used to prevent abuse are cleared automatically within days.

Children

Estimoto Plus is for vehicle owners and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, the app will show the new date here. Questions or requests about your data: support@estimoto.io.
