Shop search terms

Updated September 14, 2026

Shop search helps you locate repair businesses. Listings and vehicle suggestions do not certify workmanship, exact vehicle expertise, current opening hours or appointment availability. Confirm those details directly with the business. Distances are approximate straight-line distances from the search ZIP center, within 30 miles; results are not an exhaustive directory.

Google Maps content is subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service, incorporated here for use of that content. Its Privacy Policy describes Google's data handling. Google Maps attribution identifies its listings separately from other sources.

OpenStreetMap data is attributed to OpenStreetMap contributors under the Open Database License. Other business profiles identify their source.

Saving a dedicated shop does not contact it. Calls and website links open only when selected; participating providers receive requests only through the app's explicit request flow.
