Shop search privacy

Updated September 14, 2026

To find repair shops, Estimoto Plus sends the search ZIP, approximate ZIP location, vehicle make, selected service and search text to its directory providers. Do not enter personal information in the shop search box. Your name, email, account ID, VIN, receipts and repair photos are not included in directory searches.

When Google Maps supplies listings, its Privacy Policy applies to that processing. Google listing details are displayed for the current search and are not copied into our permanent public directory. A dedicated-shop preference stores the Google place identifier with your vehicle and chosen service; you can remove it in the app.

OpenStreetMap and Zippopotam.us supply public directory and ZIP data. We cache that public data and keep bounded request counters to manage reliability and cost. Your saved service ZIP is not changed by browsing another ZIP.
